Mysteries of the universe

Black holes, dark matter, exoplanets, cosmic origins, time, gravity, unexplained signals. This is where this site's convention matters most, because it is where an open question is most easily mistaken for an answer.

No experimental result, no observation date and no figure is published on this page. What follows describes the state of a few questions and shows how each item will carry its status. All of it is awaiting scientific review.

Dark matter

Fact, Established by observation or measurement, and not disputed in the scientific literature.

“Dark matter” is the name given to a discrepancy: the mass inferred from gravitational effects does not match the mass observed as light. The discrepancy itself is measured.

Hypothesis, A proposed explanation, consistent with observation, but not yet settled.

Several families of explanation coexist — new particles, or gravity not behaving as expected at large scale. None is settled. The name itself already assumes an answer, which is exactly the trap of this section.

Speculation, Extrapolation beyond what the data support. Published only if it is flagged.

Anything beyond that — “it must be this particle”, “gravity is wrong” — belongs in this category and will be published as such, never otherwise.

Unexplained signals

Fact, Established by observation or measurement, and not disputed in the scientific literature.

Signals whose origin has not been established are recorded, published and re-examined. That a signal is unexplained is information about the state of the analysis, not about its source.

Hypothesis, A proposed explanation, consistent with observation, but not yet settled.

An instrumental origin, a terrestrial origin and an astrophysical origin are examined before anything else, in that order, because that is the order of their frequency.

Opinion, The judgement of a named person, presented as such.

How much room to give this subject on a public platform is an editorial judgement, and it will be signed by whoever makes it.

Time and gravity

Fact, Established by observation or measurement, and not disputed in the scientific literature.

A clock's rate depends on its motion and on the gravitational field it sits in. This is not a theoretical curiosity: satellite navigation systems would not work if it were ignored.

Hypothesis, A proposed explanation, consistent with observation, but not yet settled.

How to reconcile gravity with quantum physics remains an open question, with several competing research programmes.

Deep Orbit Network working name

“Deep Orbit Network” is one of the six names proposed in chapter 12 of the specification. It is shown so that the pages can be read, not because it has been chosen. The other five are Orbital Forum, Space Frontier Lab, Cosmos R&D, Celestial Ventures and Universe Systems; the choice does not belong to the site.
