Knowledge base

A reference library rather than a news feed: glossary, timelines, diagrams, explainers and learning paths.

The six formats published

  • Short articles

    Explain a complex subject clearly.

  • Long reports

    Analyse a trend or an opportunity.

  • Forum debates

    Structure community intelligence.

  • Video capsules

    Reach a wider audience.

  • Interviews

    Bring the credibility of people who do the work.

  • Knowledge base

    Build a durable reference.

What the library will hold

  • A glossary

    Every term defined once, in one place, and linked from everywhere it appears.

  • Timelines

    They will only be published with sourced dates. An approximate timeline is the most-copied format and the hardest to correct afterwards.

  • Diagrams

    Drawn to the site's convention: solid for measured, dashed for inferred.

  • Learning paths

    A reading order, so the library is usable by someone starting out.

What remains to be validated

  • To be validated

    The search engine

    A library without search is not a library. It first requires a settled article format.

Deep Orbit Network working name

“Deep Orbit Network” is one of the six names proposed in chapter 12 of the specification. It is shown so that the pages can be read, not because it has been chosen. The other five are Orbital Forum, Space Frontier Lab, Cosmos R&D, Celestial Ventures and Universe Systems; the choice does not belong to the site.
