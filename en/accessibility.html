Accessibility

What has been done, what is measured on every build, and what could not be verified.

What has been done

  • Contrast measured in a real browser, not derived from the stylesheet. The site's lowest value is given in the README.
  • Keyboard navigation: skip link, tab order, visible focus outline, a menu that opens and closes.
  • Status labels are text, not a colour. They are read aloud and they survive printing.
  • Diagrams are decorative and hidden from screen readers; what they illustrate is always written out beside them.
  • No horizontal scrolling between 320 and 1440 pixels, checked at every breakpoint.
  • Reduced motion is respected.

What has not been done

  • The site has not been tested with a screen reader by someone who uses one daily. Automated checks do not replace that.
  • No conformity statement is published. A statement implies an audit, and none has taken place.

Deep Orbit Network working name

“Deep Orbit Network” is one of the six names proposed in chapter 12 of the specification. It is shown so that the pages can be read, not because it has been chosen. The other five are Orbital Forum, Space Frontier Lab, Cosmos R&D, Celestial Ventures and Universe Systems; the choice does not belong to the site.
