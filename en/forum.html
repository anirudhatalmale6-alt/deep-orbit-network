The forum

A moderated community for discussing rockets, satellites, astronomy, missions, unexplained phenomena and future exploration.

The ten categories

  • Rockets and propulsion
  • Space agencies and missions
  • Satellites, telecom and Earth observation
  • Astronomy and deep-space discoveries
  • Mysteries of the universe and scientific hypotheses
  • Space robotics and autonomous systems
  • AI and software for space operations
  • Space law, geopolitics and regulation
  • Startup ideas, patents and investment opportunities
  • Student projects, competitions and education

The rules, written before the first post

  • A scientific claim carries its status or it gets reworded. This is not a courtesy: it is what makes disagreement possible.
  • A source beats a certainty. A post that cites what it rests on is more useful than a post that happens to be right.
  • No financial solicitation. No fundraising, no offer, no private message proposing an investment.
  • Speculation is allowed and flagged. It is not censored, it is labelled.
  • Expert labels are verified or they do not exist. An “engineer” badge nobody checked is worse than none.
  • Attack an argument, never a person.

What triggers a review

  • A technical claim presented as settled without a source.
  • A post touching money: fundraising, valuation, returns.
  • A request for an expert label.
  • A misinformation report from another member.

Deep Orbit Network working name

“Deep Orbit Network” is one of the six names proposed in chapter 12 of the specification. It is shown so that the pages can be read, not because it has been chosen. The other five are Orbital Forum, Space Frontier Lab, Cosmos R&D, Celestial Ventures and Universe Systems; the choice does not belong to the site.
